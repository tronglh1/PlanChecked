## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
Vườn Nhà keeps your home plants on track with the right task at the right time, whether you are a first-time grower or experienced. Completely free, no ads.

PICK A PLANT, GET A PLAN
Search vegetables, fruit plants and ornamentals (Vietnamese names with or without diacritics). The app builds a stage-by-stage care plan that fits the season, your climate zone (North / Central / South), your space (balcony, rooftop, indoors) and sunlight. You review and edit it before it starts.

WHAT TO DO TODAY
The Today screen groups tasks by time of day: water, feed, prune, check. Done, Skip or Snooze in one tap. It works offline and syncs when you are back online.

REMINDERS AT THE RIGHT TIME
Notifications follow the time windows you choose, with quiet hours so you are not disturbed.

PHOTO CHECK-INS WITH AI SUGGESTIONS
Take a photo and AI estimates the growth stage, spots signs such as yellow leaves, wilting, pests or nutrient issues, and proposes plan adjustments. You decide which changes to apply. AI suggestions are for guidance only.

GROW TOGETHER
Invite family with a code or link, share projects, assign tasks and see who watered the plants today.

FUN REWARDS
Earn experience points, badges and streaks for steady care.

PRIVACY
We do not sell your data. No GPS needed: you only pick your province. Delete your account and all data right in the app.
```
